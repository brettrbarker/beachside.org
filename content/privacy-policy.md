Beachside Church respects your privacy. This static website does not require an account and does not directly store form submissions or payment information.

Links for giving, registration, connection, and prayer may take you to third-party services such as Church Center. Those services process information under their own privacy and security policies. Embedded media providers may receive standard browser information when you play a video.

Standard server logs may record technical details such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, and time of access for security and reliability purposes. We do not sell personal information.

For questions about this policy or information you have shared with Beachside, contact [admin@beachside.org](mailto:admin@beachside.org) or call [850.629.9491](tel:+18506299491).
